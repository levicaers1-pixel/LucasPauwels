About Lucas

From the first swing at age 8, I knew golf was my calling.

Now, as a dedicated professional on the rise, I'm committed to pushing the boundaries of my game and achieving excellence on the green. My journey has been one of relentless pursuit—of perfection, of growth, and of the next great shot.

With multiple amateur championships under my belt and a rapidly growing reputation in the professional circuit, I'm not just playing the game—I'm redefining it. Every tournament is a new opportunity to prove that hard work, strategy, and passion can overcome any challenge.
